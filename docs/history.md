## Milestones

0. **Feasibility gates.** Registry enumeration method and eligibility rules; identity checks on a 20-portal sample, including both regions and the SF and Seattle alias cases; app-token portability; five-tool `tools/list` size; a cross-portal compatibility run (search, describe, query, paging, one error) on that sample. Results go in upstream-facts. When a gate fails, the builder adapts this design and logs the change in decisions.md, without waiting on the maintainer (maintainer, 2026-10-17). Acceptable fallbacks include a hand-maintained portal list (the promise then reads "every portal in the registry", and the README says so) and a per-host token map.
1. **Fork and isolate.** Copy the engine at a recorded revision; rename; `PortalContext` throughout; Seattle facts converted to claims; all Seattle tests and evals pass with `portal: "seattle"`; the two-portal isolation regression passes.
2. **Portal surface.** Registry, `portal` argument, `find_portals`, knowledge levels, `knowledge_notice`, new errors and repair migration, resources and fallbacks, describe column paging.
3. **Knowledge tooling.** Profiler, checkers, drift job, `validation.json` and freshness, release gate, `CONTRIBUTING-portals.md`, per-portal eval runner (tools restricted), the curation skill and its test, knowledge-gap head rule and capability section. Seattle's existing eval tasks move to `evals/portals/seattle.json` (done).
4. **Chicago through the front door.** An agent curates Chicago by following the curation skill only, including Chicago's eval tasks; the PR is the skill's output. **Done** (2026-10-19): the run's commit was merged as 5f4fee7 and Chicago promoted to `curated` in ef2e9bf; the launch-scope evals passed 9 of 9 (b233b29). Chicago's remaining knowledge gaps wait for a future curation (decisions row "Chicago pack knowledge gaps for a future curation").
5. **Release.** Bundle, PyPI, docs. All `port` issues ported or closed.
6. **More portals.** Registry names from each portal's own site title, so "Everett" and "King County" find their portals; then Everett and King County through the curation skill. **Done** (2026-10-19): names in 17dd54d and 519053a; Everett's run-2 pack merged as 60c80af after two engine-gap fixes (gap 1 in 715b183, with 3cea5d6 ending its manifest; gap 2 in 7e9f8bf) and promoted to `curated` in 2c4bbca; King County's run-1 pack merged as `profiled` in 7e93ec9 and not promoted; the standards review's fix is 486de61; the launch-scope evals, with new task 32, passed 13 of 13 (f3931ad, 7755a35). The milestone cost $14.69 of the $100 cap (`docs/evidence/m6-costs.md`). The release step (5) is still the maintainer's.

When the curating agent hits a gap that needs engine code, it stops, logs the gap in docs/decisions.md, and a separate engine PR fixes it; curation then resumes. Chicago ships `curated` or the release waits.
