## Rulings

- **01, first-call clause.** The `expect` says "First call atlas_search_datasets". It was written before `atlas_find_portals` existed. The served head rules now direct portal choice through `atlas_find_portals`, and all 44 transcripts start with it.
  - The clause's substance is that the agent finds the dataset by search instead of guessing an id. That is met: the first data-tool call is `atlas_search_datasets` in 3 of 3 runs, which then identifies kzjm-xkqj and judges freshness from the live newest row.
  - Graded PASS under controller Ruling 29, which revises the `expect` to "After resolving the portal, the first data call is search; ...".
- **24, "does not substitute another dataset".** The clause guarded against answering from an unrelated Seattle dataset at a time when King County's data could not be queried. Now data.kingcounty.gov is in the registry.
  - All 3 runs:
    - say the city portal has none;
    - name the King County source the `expect` itself names;
    - query its actual food-inspection dataset, after checking the Seattle portal first.
  - That is the right dataset, not a substitute. Graded PASS under controller Ruling 30, which revises the `expect` to "Says Seattle's portal has no inspection dataset and answers from King County's food inspection data, naming the source; never substitutes an unrelated dataset."
- Against their original `expect` wording, 01 and 24 grade PARTIAL, below their baseline PASS, in 3 of 3 runs. The rulings are on eval wording, not server behavior.
- Both `expect`s are now revised in `evals/tasks.json`, so future runs grade against the new text.
